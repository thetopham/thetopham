## About

I’m Matt, a computer science student at CU Boulder. I’m building AI tools that bring stories to life, help me remember my day, and run on computers I manage at home.

Marinara is where the creative side comes together. We already write scripts every day in our roleplays—why not turn them into animation and anime? I’m working on a MiniMax H3 workflow that uses character-sheet images and written personas to keep characters recognizable, plus World Maps reference images to keep locations consistent. AI turns the narration into a scene-by-scene plan and keyframes, then ComfyUI brings the images and motion instructions together for animation. The dream is an anime that grows with the story. It’s still a work in progress.

Manfred is my wearable memory project: an Omi wearable for daily audio transcription and searchable notes, plus E09 smart glasses connected to ChatGPT Live for voice and photo sharing. The glasses work for voice; I’m still improving how reliably photos get captured and sent.

Demerzel is the helper behind the scenes. It brings together daily AI briefs, a personal wiki built from daily conversations, and management of my home AI computers: an AMD Radeon AI PRO R9700 system, a dual RTX 3060 system, and a single RTX 3060 system. Its tools check what is running, start and stop supported AI programs, and keep jobs from competing for the same graphics card.

I’ve contributed 100 merged pull requests across Marinara Engine and Agents, verified September 5, 2026. I also build web and VR learning experiments through School of the Ancients.

I’m interested in software and AI development opportunities where I can turn an ambitious idea into something people can actually use.
